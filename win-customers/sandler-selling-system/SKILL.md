# Sandler Selling System

The Sandler system keeps the seller out of the prospect's system. Use the **Submarine**: seal each compartment before moving forward, keep pressure low, and let the prospect discover, qualify, and close.

## Core Principle

Do not pitch your way into control. Use contracts and questions to make the prospect's truth visible: pain, money, decision, then fulfillment. If pain, budget, or decision is missing, do not present as if the deal is qualified.

## Run Sequence

1. **Choose the branch.** Classify the request as call plan, deal diagnosis, script/objection, roleplay, or drill. Completion: the answer follows that branch rather than generic sales advice.
2. **Locate the Submarine compartment.** Identify the current stage: bonding, contract, pain, budget, decision, fulfillment, or post-sell. Completion: name the compartment and whether it is sealed, leaky, or skipped.
3. **Protect the seller's I/R.** Treat rejection, stalls, and awkward money/decision talk as role feedback, not identity threat. Completion: the response stays calm, adult, and non-needy.
4. **Use questions before claims.** Prefer dummying up, reversing, and upfront contracts before explanation or persuasion. Completion: no pitch appears before pain, budget, and decision are accounted for.
5. **Advance by contract.** End with a clear yes, no, or next contract; avoid mutual mystification. Completion: next step states who does what by when and what happens after.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Pitching before pain | Creates unpaid consulting. | Dummy up and reverse into pain. |
| Weak upfront contract | Leaves the prospect's system in control. | Contract time, agenda, yes/no, and next step. |
| Avoiding money | Lets price ambush the close. | Ask budget in round numbers after pain. |
| Accepting “think it over” | Creates pressure and ghosting. | Qualify decision before fulfillment. |
| Over-presenting | Adds irrelevant seagulls to the prospect's picture. | Fulfill only agreed pain. |
| Leaving after the signature | Lets buyer's remorse work alone. | Post-sell before leaving. |
