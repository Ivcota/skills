# Gap Sales Leadership

Use this reference for sales managers, pipeline reviews, forecast coaching, commit culture, hiring, and team implementation.

Source refs: *Gap Selling*, Ch. 18-21; see [`sources.md`](sources.md).

## Pipeline Review

A gap pipeline review is not a status update. It verifies whether the rep has enough information and influence to forecast and move the deal.

Managers should confirm:

1. Current state.
2. Future state.
3. Gap.
4. Intrinsic motivation.
5. Decision criteria.
6. Evidence that the information is accurate.
7. What happens next and when.

### Review questions

| Area | Manager asks |
|---|---|
| Current state | “What are the literal facts? Problems? Impact? Root cause? Emotional state?” |
| Future state | “What outcome do they want? What changes if they get it?” |
| Gap | “How did you calculate the gap?” |
| Motivation | “Why does this matter to them now?” |
| Criteria | “How will they decide?” |
| Process | “Who is involved and what steps remain?” |
| Next yes | “What buyer commitment moves this forward?” |
| Evidence | “Did they say this, or do you think it?” |

Completion bar: if the rep cannot answer with specifics, the action is to return to the buyer, not invent strategy.

## Insist on Specific Data

Reject generic claims:

- “They want to grow faster.”
- “They liked the demo.”
- “The deal looks good.”
- “They should decide soon.”

Require specifics:

- Grow from what to what, by when?
- What problem did the demo solve?
- What next yes did the buyer commit to?
- Who decides and what criteria do they use?

Completion bar: the CRM note should be specific enough to pass the CRM Challenge.

## Next Yes in Reviews

The next yes is a customer commitment. It is not a rep task.

Manager prompt:

- “What is the customer's next yes?”
- “Why would the customer value saying yes to that?”
- “What information or proof do they need to say yes?”
- “What could block that yes?”

Completion bar: every forecasted deal has a current or upcoming next yes.

## Healthy Pipeline Checklist

A pipeline is healthy when reps can provide:

- Accurate close dates within a reasonable window.
- Accurate quarterly commits within about 15% on either side.
- Clear next steps / next yes.
- Clear deal strategies.
- Enough pipeline coverage to meet commit and quota.
- Evidence for all of the above.

## Commit Culture

Commit culture means reps take ownership of the number the data supports. It values accuracy over optimism.

Rules:

1. Ask reps for their commit.
2. Let them commit to the number they believe is real.
3. Challenge the data gently.
4. Accept the number after challenge.
5. Use gaps between commit and quota early enough to fix pipeline.

Key distinction:

- **Quota** is the goal.
- **Commit** is the evidence-based forecast.

Do not force a rep to raise commit to match quota. That destroys trust and prediction quality.

Completion bar: the team can see shortfall early enough to act before end-of-quarter panic.

## Hiring Traits

Gap sellers need different traits than traditional pitch-heavy sellers.

Hire for:

| Trait | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Curiosity** | Drives deep questions and real diagnosis. |
| **Critical thinking** | Interprets information and challenges assumptions. |
| **Empathy** | Lets buyers feel understood and safe enough to share. |
| **Problem-solving** | Makes selling about fixing, not pitching. |
| **Leadership** | Helps the seller guide change confidently. |
| **Creativity** | Finds paths through messy problems and objections. |
| **Deliberate learning** | Keeps the seller's expertise current. |
| **Coachability** | Allows skill improvement and feedback uptake. |
| **Business acumen** | Enables credible diagnosis of business impact. |

Completion bar: hiring decisions should weigh gap-selling traits more than charm, tenure, or network alone.

## Coaching Culture

A gap sales leader is a coach. The job is to reinforce the methodology through:

- Pipeline meetings.
- CRM inspection.
- Deal coaching.
- Hiring.
- Reviews.
- Repetition and practice.

The team must learn to manage to current state, future state, gap, and next yes. Training without ongoing reinforcement will not stick.
