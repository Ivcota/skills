## 3. Qualification

Answer yes/no:

1. Does the prospect have a problem we can fix?
2. Does the prospect agree they have it?
3. Does the prospect want to fix it?
4. Will the prospect go on a journey with us to fix it?

If any no: widen, hold, or exit.
