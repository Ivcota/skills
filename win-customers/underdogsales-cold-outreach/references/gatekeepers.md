# Gatekeeper Framework — UnderdogSales Cold Outreach

Source: user-provided UnderdogSales framework notes, 2026-06-19.

Use this branch when the user needs to reach a prospect through a receptionist, assistant, or gatekeeper.

## Branch 1: Power through

Use when speed and authority matter. Keep answers short; do not over-explain.

Gatekeeper: “Who is calling?”

Seller: “It's {surname}, thanks.”

Gatekeeper: “What company are you calling from?”

Seller: “{acronym}, thank you.”

Gatekeeper: “What is this call regarding?”

Seller: “{technical-enough reason they probably won't know}.”

Gatekeeper: “Are they expecting your call?”

Seller: “I'd expect so.”

## Branch 2: Get help

Use when rapport with the gatekeeper is more useful than pushing through.

Seller: “Hi, who am I speaking with?”

Gatekeeper: “Mark. Who am I speaking to?”

Seller: “Hi Mark, my name is {name}, and I need to speak with {prospect name}. Can you put me through?”

Gatekeeper: “What is this regarding?”

Seller: “I help {job titles} fix {problems}; that's what {prospect} takes care of, right?”

Gatekeeper: “That's right.”

Seller: “Do you mind putting me through? Thanks.”

## Guardrails

- Do not give a full pitch to the gatekeeper.
- Do not let the gatekeeper become the discovery call unless the user explicitly wants that.
- Keep the ask simple: speak to the prospect.
