# Scoring Rubrics

Universal 1–10 bands for each lever. **Score perception**, not just reality *(book)* — value lands when the prospect *perceives* the four drivers shift.

Cite evidence from intake to justify the score. Use **Calibrating examples by type** at the bottom for the artifact domain.

## Driver 1: Dream Outcome *(book — Goal: Increase)*

The feelings and experiences they already want. **Channel desire; do not invent it** *(book)*. Bigger, more specific, more emotional, more **status-relevant to their peer group** = higher score.

**Sell the vacation, not the plane flight** *(book)* — Dream Outcome is them **at the destination**, living what they want to **experience** (e.g. *lose 20 lbs in 6 weeks* as the challenge prize — not “gym membership,” not the delivery vehicle). Membership, coaching, and software are the flight; the named end state + timeframe is the vacation.

When comparing **different** desires (e.g. wealth vs beauty for an avatar), the outcome that raises **status** in *their* group wins. When comparing **two vehicles for the same desire**, Dream scores tie — use the other three levers.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1-2 | Vague, generic, low emotional charge |
| 3-4 | Somewhat specific, moderate desire |
| 5-6 | Specific and desirable, but common |
| 7-8 | Highly specific, emotionally charged, quantified or vividly named |
| 9-10 | Life-changing, deeply emotional, identity-shifting, uniquely articulated; framed as **status gain in others’ eyes** when copy-relevant |

## Driver 2: Perceived Likelihood of Achievement *(book — Goal: Increase)*

*(Do not use “Likelihood” alone with the user without “of Achievement” at least once in the thread.)*

**Certainty** — “How likely is it this works for *me* if I buy?” *(book)*. Same deliverable, different conviction (e.g. surgeon’s 10,000th patient vs first) = different value. Proof, messaging, what you include/exclude, guarantees = higher **perceived** likelihood.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1-2 | No proof, no system, no precedent |
| 3-4 | Thin proof or vague process |
| 5-6 | Solid proof + clear methodology |
| 7-8 | Extensive proof, named system, partial guarantee or track record |
| 9-10 | Overwhelming proof, performance guarantee, public metrics |

## Driver 3: Time Delay *(book — Perceived Time Delay Between Start and Achievement; Goal: Decrease)*

Gap between commit and **promised** benefit. Two layers *(book)*:

1. **Long-term** — the dream outcome timeline they’re buying.
2. **Short-term** — milestones and emotional wins *along the way* that keep them in until the long result lands.

**Perceived** delay counts (e.g. dotted map vs faster trains). This sits on the denominator. Drive toward **1** in chat; do not score 0.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Immediate or same-session result; perceived “now” |
| 2 | Fast first win (Day 1 / Hour 1), full result soon after |
| 3-4 | Moderate, with clear milestones |
| 5-6 | Slow, faint quick wins |
| 7-10 | Long delay, no early signals |

## Driver 4: Effort & Sacrifice *(book — Goal: Decrease)*

**Ancillary costs** along the way — tangible and intangible *(book)*: time, money, discomfort, embarrassment, identity, fear of impermanence. **Done-for-you** usually beats DIY on both Effort and Likelihood. Denominator — drive toward **1**; do not score 0.

Competing against **free**? **Fast beats free** *(book)* — score Time and Effort through speed and friction removal, not price alone.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Zero effort, fully done-for-them, no sacrifice |
| 2 | Low effort, mostly done-for-them |
| 3-4 | Moderate effort, clear instructions, some automation |
| 5-6 | Significant effort, some hand-holding |
| 7-10 | High effort, steep curve, major sacrifice |

## Value score *(workflow — the composite)*

**Formula:** `(Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`

Perfect: (10 × 10) / (1 × 1) = **100**.
Poor: (2 × 2) / (9 × 9) ≈ **0.05**.

| Composite | Band | Meaning |
|-----------|------|---------|
| 50-100 | **Grand Slam** *(book)* | Premium pricing/attention/adoption justified. Focus on distribution, not the artifact. |
| 20-49 | **Strong** | Good. One or two levers worth optimizing. |
| 5-19 | **Average** | Multiple levers need work. Likely to compete on price/attention. |
| Below 5 | **Weak** | Fundamental redesign needed before more spend or distribution. |

## Diagnosis rules

- **Weakest driver** = largest distance from its best end. Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood of Achievement: `10 − score`. Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice: `score − 1`.
- On ties, pick **Time Delay** or **Effort & Sacrifice** — the bottom of the value equation *(book)*.
- **Dream Outcome below 5** — clarify the dream before other drivers.
- **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement below 5** with no audience yet — proof gap; first move is proof, not louder claims.
- **Top of equation maxed but commodity claims** — *get the bottom to zero* and make perceived likelihood *felt* *(book)*.

## Comparing vehicles *(book — optional diagnostic)*

When the user names two **vehicles** with the **same** dream outcome (e.g. meditation vs Xanax, fitness vs liposuction, DIY vs done-for-you):

| Driver | Score each vehicle |
|--------|-------------------|
| Dream Outcome | 1 if promise matches, else 0 (usually both 1) |
| Perceived Likelihood of Achievement | 1 if prospect believes this vehicle works for them |
| Time Delay | 1 if faster **perceived** path (use .5 if partial, per book) |
| Effort & Sacrifice | 1 if clearly less sacrifice |

Sum each side (max 4). Use to explain price gaps, not as a second 1–10 rubric.

## Calibrating examples by artifact type

Use these to anchor the universal bands in the artifact's domain. Read the row matching the user's artifact type before scoring.

### OFFER (paid product/service/package/course/coaching)

| Lever | Best end | Middle | Worst end |
|-------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Dream Outcome (toward 10) | "Replace your 9-to-5 income in 120 days and never miss another school pickup" | "Generate 50 qualified leads/month" | "Improve your marketing" |
| Likelihood (toward 10) | 500+ case studies, performance guarantee, public dashboard | 20+ testimonials + named framework | "Trust me, this works" |
| Time Delay (toward 1) | Set up in 1 hour, first leads same day | First result in 30 days, full in 90 | Results in 6+ months, no quick wins |
| Effort (toward 1) | Done-for-you; 3 decisions total | 10-step playbook with templates | 200-hour cert + DIY implementation |

### CONTENT (video/essay/post/talk/thread/podcast)

| Lever | Best end | Middle | Worst end |
|-------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Dream Outcome (toward 10) | A reframe that changes how the viewer sees X for years | "You'll learn how X works" | Vague "useful info" |
| Likelihood (toward 10) | Specific case + numbers + named track record + receipts shown | Author bio + 1-2 examples | No credibility, no examples |
| Time Delay (toward 1) | First insight in first 3 seconds; one quotable line per minute | Hook by 30s, payoff by minute 2 | Payoff in last paragraph after long preamble |
| Effort (toward 1) | Single read; no jargon; visual aids do the heavy lifting | Skimmable with headers and visuals | Dense, requires re-reading, demands prerequisites |

### FEATURE (capability/tool/component inside a product)

| Lever | Best end | Middle | Worst end |
|-------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Dream Outcome (toward 10) | "Ship the deal — every objection auto-handled, contract auto-drafted, nothing falls through" | "Send a report in 3 clicks" | "Manage your X" |
| Likelihood (toward 10) | Live preview, success stories in-product, real-time success counter | Sample data, helper text, in-app docs | Empty UI, no examples, no signal |
| Time Delay (toward 1) | Value within 10 seconds of opening; zero-config | Value within first session after onboarding | First value after multi-step setup and config |
| Effort (toward 1) | Invisible — works the way the user already thinks | Familiar pattern, light learning | Forces a new mental model |

### IDEA (concept/strategy/pitch/hypothesis/framework)

| Lever | Best end | Middle | Worst end |
|-------|----------|--------|-----------|
| Dream Outcome (toward 10) | Reframes a category — if true, makes existing alternatives look obsolete | "Solves a known problem for a real segment" | "It'd be cool if..." |
| Likelihood (toward 10) | Adjacent precedents + early signal (waitlist, paid pilot, organic pull) | Adjacent precedents exist | No precedent, no analogies |
| Time Delay (toward 1) | Signal achievable this week with a 1-day test | Signal in weeks | Years to first signal |
| Effort (toward 1) | Test runnable today with no engineering | Requires a small experiment | Requires building a platform first |
