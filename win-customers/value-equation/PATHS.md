# Paths to the best score

Each value driver has a ladder *(workflow)*. Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood of Achievement climb toward 10. Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice descend toward 1. A 3 cannot jump to a 9, and a 6 cannot jump to a 2. Below each ladder are **type anchors**: how that rung looks for an offer / content / feature / idea.

In **evaluate mode**, use ladders to prescribe the next move from the user's current score.
In **create mode**, use ladders as a **candidate menu** at each lever — surface 2-3 rungs near the best end and let the user pick.

## Psychological vs logical *(book)*

Most people default to **logical** fixes (faster product, cheaper price). Those are often already tried. Prefer **psychological** moves when they change *perception* of Time, Effort, or Likelihood without proportional delivery cost:

| Logical (commodity) | Psychological (perception) |
|---------------------|----------------------------|
| Make trains faster | Dotted map / countdown — less *felt* wait |
| Make elevator faster | Mirrors — distraction from wait |
| Discount to compete | Fewer units, higher price — desire |
| Bigger dream claim | Show others noticing their status win |

Offer a psychological rung when the user’s space is crowded on claims or features.

## Dream Outcome — increase *(book: Goal = Increase)*

**Lock check** *(book)* — state the dream in one line: **[result] in [timeframe]**. Ask: “Is this the **vacation** (destination + experience) or the **plane flight** (membership, calls, access)?” If it’s the vehicle, rewrite to the arrival/experience only — Time Delay gets its own driver later (e.g. *6 weeks* belongs in the dream headline and in Time Delay, not buried in “unlimited support”).

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Pick a single audience | Choose one segment. Kill the rest. | 1 day | 3 |
| 3 | Get their words | Listen/interview/read 10 of them. Capture verbatim phrases for "ideal end state." | 1 week | 5 |
| 5 | Quantify it | Attach a number and a time frame to the outcome. | 2 hours | 6 |
| 6 | Add emotional layer | Rewrite as: "Go from [painful state] to [dream state]." Name the feeling. | 1 hour | 7 |
| 7 | Add identity layer | Write who they become: "the founder who...", "the parent who...", "the engineer who..." | 1 hour | 8 |
| 8 | Benchmark against best result | Anchor headline on your top 1% outcome, with proof. | 1 week | 9 |
| 9 | Uniquely articulate | Phrase the outcome so no competitor can copy: domain-specific + time-specific + emotion-specific. | 1 day | 10 |
| — | **Status-from-others** *(book)* | Rewrite benefit: how peers/partner/boss *see* them after the win. One concrete scene. | 1 hour | +1 perception |

**Why it matters:** Dream Outcome is the ceiling on price/attention/adoption. No other lever compensates for a weak "what." When two offers share the same promise, stop here and work Likelihood / Time / Effort.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** quantified revenue/time/identity outcome ("$10K MRR in 90 days")
- **CONTENT:** the reframe — name the belief shift the audience leaves with ("X is not Y, it's Z")
- **FEATURE:** the verb the user accomplishes in one beat ("ship the deal," "close the loop")
- **IDEA:** the category-resetting claim ("If this is true, [existing thing] is obsolete")

## Perceived Likelihood of Achievement — increase *(book: Goal = Increase)*

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Get 3 free users / pilots | Free trial in exchange for testimonials and data. | 2-6 weeks | 3 |
| 3 | Ship 3 case studies / receipts | Names, metrics, before/after, timeline. Publish. | 1 week | 5 |
| 5 | Name your system | Give the process a proprietary name. Document the steps. | 1 day | 6 |
| 6 | Add a guarantee or proof bar | Money-back, satisfaction, performance, or a hard data point on the page. *Communicate* likelihood in messaging — proof they can’t unsee. | 1 day | 7 |
| 7 | Scale to 20+ proof assets | Testimonials + case studies + screenshots + data + credentials. Stack. | 1-3 months | 8 |
| 8 | Performance / outcome guarantee | "If you don't hit X, we keep working free / refund double / repay your time." | planning + legal | 9 |
| 9 | Publish running metrics | Public dashboard, success rate, total impact for users. Live. | ongoing | 10 |

**Why it matters:** The audience is always asking "will this work for *me*?" Every rung lowers that doubt.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** testimonials, case studies, money-back / performance guarantees
- **CONTENT:** show your work — data tables, source links, real screenshots, "I tested this on N tries" credibility
- **FEATURE:** in-product proof — sample data, success stories visible, live counters, "10K teams use this"
- **IDEA:** signed LOIs, waitlist count, pilot results, adjacent precedents

## Time Delay — decrease *(book: Goal = Decrease)*

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 9-10 | Design a "Day 1 Win" | One tangible emotional win as close to purchase as possible *(book: fast wins)*. | 1 week | 8 |
| — | **Perceived wait** *(book)* | Progress UI, roadmap, “you are here,” ETA — shrink felt delay without changing core timeline. | 2 days | −1 perceived |
| 8 | Break journey into milestones | Week 1 / 2 / 4 deliverables so progress is continuous, not back-loaded. | 3 days | 6 |
| 6 | Add done-for-them setup | You (or the system) do onboarding/migration instead of the user. | 1-4 weeks | 5 |
| 5 | Ship templates and starting points | Eliminate "blank page" — pre-fill everything possible. | 2 weeks | 4 |
| 4 | Automate non-input steps | Anything that doesn't need their brain — automate. | variable | 3 |
| 3 | Compress Day 1 Win to Hour 1 | Cut everything between commit and first result. | 2 weeks | 2 |
| 2 | Same-session full delivery | User goes from commit to full payoff in a single session. | redesign | 1 |

**Why it matters:** Time Delay is on the bottom of the Value Equation. Cutting it from 6 to 3 doubles the value score. *Get the bottom to zero* — 1 is the chat cap.

**Fast beats free** *(book)* — if competing with free: expedited lane, same-day start, concierge onboarding, “live in 7 days” — charge for speed.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** first result in days, not months; concierge onboarding
- **CONTENT:** insight in the first 3 seconds; one payoff per minute; no preamble
- **FEATURE:** zero-config, value in 10 seconds, defaults pre-selected
- **IDEA:** smallest test runnable this week (not next quarter)

## Effort & Sacrifice — decrease *(book: Goal = Decrease)*

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 9-10 | Map every step | List every action the user must take. Count them. | 2 hours | 8 |
| 8 | Replace "learn then do" with "do with guidance" | Live support, concierge, co-working, voiceover walkthroughs. | 1 week | 6 |
| 6 | Provide fill-in-the-blank skeletons | Replace blank exercises with pre-filled defaults. | 1-2 weeks | 5 |
| 5 | Pre-select defaults | Every decision they'd have to make — make it for them with a recommended option. | 3 days | 4 |
| 4 | Done-with-you | You're in the loop; they approve decisions. | operational | 3 |
| 3 | Done-for-you | They provide inputs once; you/system execute everything. | major redesign | 2 |
| — | **List sacrifice** *(book)* | Enumerate every hassle the old path required; show your path deleting each line (lipo vs gym style). | 2 hours | clarity |
| 2 | Address identity + social cost | Remove the "I have to become someone else / be seen differently" barrier. | 1 week | 1 |

**Why it matters:** Effort is the other denominator. Done-for-you is the single biggest value multiplier. The book's zero is the direction; 1 is the score.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** done-for-you delivery, zero decisions, single input call
- **CONTENT:** no jargon, no prerequisites, single read, visuals do the work
- **FEATURE:** invisible — works the way the user already thinks; no new mental model
- **IDEA:** test requires no engineering, no new platform, no team change

## Choosing the rung

- Dream and Likelihood: pick the smallest rung above the current score, toward 10. Time Delay and Effort: pick the smallest rung below the current score, toward 1. Do not skip.
- Prefer **denominator improvements** (Time Delay, Effort) for compounding impact once Dream is clear *(book: get the bottom to zero)*. Lower is the improvement.
- Prefer **Dream Outcome** fixes if the score is below 5 — nothing else matters until the "what" is clear.
- Prefer **Perceived Likelihood** fixes if there's an audience but it isn't converting — the gap is usually trust, not desire.

## First-week / first-build action rules

The "this week" action (evaluate mode) or "first build action" (create mode) must satisfy all three:

1. **Takes ≤5 hours total** — it has to actually happen.
2. **Produces a visible artifact** — a doc, a page, a draft, a template, a screen recording.
3. **Moves exactly one lever one rung toward its best end** — up for Dream and Likelihood, down for Time Delay and Effort. No multitasking across levers.

If the action doesn't fit all three, downscope it.
