# Value Equation

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice).** *(book)* Division, not addition: shrinking the bottom multiplies value. The hypothetical limit is **instant dream outcome, zero effort and sacrifice** — chat caps Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice at **1** so the value score stays defined. Perfect = (10 × 10) / (1 × 1) = 100.

**Four drivers:** increase Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood of Achievement; decrease Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice. Score each 1–10 toward those goals (Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice: lower is better).

**Buyer questions** *(book — map every move to one)*:

| They ask | Driver |
|----------|--------|
| What will I make? | Dream Outcome |
| How will I know it’s going to happen? | Perceived Likelihood of Achievement |
| How long will it take? | Time Delay |
| What is expected of me? | Effort & Sacrifice |

**Perception is reality** *(book)* — score and design what the prospect *perceives*, not only objective truth. Communicate each driver so they feel the shift.

**When two vehicles promise the same dream outcome**, Dream Outcome value cancels; Perceived Likelihood of Achievement, Time Delay, and Effort & Sacrifice set price *(book — meditation vs Xanax)*.

**Serial** *(workflow)* means one driver is active per turn.

Load [RUBRICS.md](RUBRICS.md), [PATHS.md](PATHS.md), and [VOCABULARY.md](VOCABULARY.md). Read only the active driver's band and ladder. When **Dollars** runs, load [DOLLARS.md](DOLLARS.md) and follow **trigger routing** (Audit / Stack / Ratio).

## How to speak

The first lines of every turn are the answer, in normal sentences. Use the **book names** for the four value drivers (see VOCABULARY.md). After the reader knows the framework, you may shorten *Perceived Likelihood of Achievement* to *perceived likelihood* — not “belief” or “certainty” alone without tying to achievement.

*(workflow)* Words like rung, lock, ledger, CREATE, EVALUATE stay in this file — not for the user.

Put the ledger in one line underneath the answer, for the next turn. Do not lead with it.

## Feasibility *(workflow — was “Possible”)*

Before you name a new score for a change, both must be true:

1. The seller can actually do the move.
2. The move does not weaken a part that is already locked.

If either fails, the score stays, and you say why in one sentence. A guarantee, a scarce spot, and a refund still need a yes before they lock. So does any other promise the seller must deliver.

## Intake

Ask both before designing or scoring. If the conversation already answered one, confirm it in one line and ask only the open one.

1. **Mode** — evaluate something that exists, or create something new?
2. **Type** — offer, content, feature, or idea?

Carry a **ledger** every turn after intake:

```
LOCKED: Dream Outcome — OPEN | Perceived Likelihood — OPEN | Time Delay — OPEN | Effort & Sacrifice — OPEN
ACTIVE: <one driver or evaluate step>
```

A driver is **LOCKED** only when the user picked or edited a move, a target score is stated, and every gate on that driver is answered. Unanswered gates stay **OPEN**. Do not invent locks.

**Output is conversation-only.** Save `./value-equation-briefs/<slug>.md` only if the user says "save the brief" or the workspace is already a project directory they asked to write in.

## Overload

If the user says the turn is too much, "one at a time," or similar: restate the ledger, the active driver in one sentence, and **one question**. No second driver, no value score math, no tables with more than three rows.

If the user says **"answer directly," "just the number," "stop confusing,"** or similar: lead with the direct answer in one sentence; at most one short follow-up block. In **Dollars**, use **Audit** mode per [DOLLARS.md](DOLLARS.md).

## CREATE *(workflow — design a new offer or artifact)*

Order: **Dream Outcome → Perceived Likelihood of Achievement → Time Delay → Effort & Sacrifice**.

### One turn, one driver

On **Dream Outcome** only: apply PATHS.md lock check — vacation (destination + experience), not the plane flight *(book)*.

1. Quote the best band for this type from RUBRICS.md: 9–10 for Dream and Likelihood, 1 for Time Delay and Effort.
2. Offer **at most three** concrete moves from PATHS.md for this lever. Prefer **psychological** moves when the logical fix is commoditized *(book — PATHS.md → Psychological vs logical)*. Extra rungs stay in PATHS.md until the user asks for more.
3. Ask **one** lock question (pick, edit, or their own).
4. If the move is a promise you must honor (guarantee, scarcity, refund), the lock question is the honor gate. **Yes** locks it. **Not yet** stays OPEN and you soften that move next turn. Do not proceed to the next driver while a gate is OPEN.

**Until Effort & Sacrifice is LOCKED, omit:** value score math, weakest-driver diagnosis, "what we do next" forks, and previews of later drivers.

### After all four drivers are LOCKED

Then stop.

1. Say the offer using the four driver names in plain sentences.
2. Compute `(Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`. Name the band from RUBRICS.md.
3. Name the **weakest driver** (largest distance from best end; tie → pick Time Delay or Effort & Sacrifice).
4. One line on **perception is reality**: what the prospect must *perceive* for the value to land.
5. Put the ledger in one line under that. Do not open the next driver.

**Compare two vehicles** *(book — only if user asks)*: same dream outcome; binary 0/1 per driver (RUBRICS.md → Comparing vehicles).

A later turn changes a number only if the user asks. Run **Feasibility** first. A detail inside a locked score does not reopen that driver. Say "that is already in the score" in one line.

### Dollars *(optional)*

Run only if the user asks what it is worth, what to charge, or to stack the value. Do not start on your own.

1. Load [DOLLARS.md](DOLLARS.md).
2. **Route** by user intent: **Audit** | **Stack** | **Ratio** (table in DOLLARS.md). State the mode in one line.
3. Complete that mode's steps; **stop** when its completion criterion is met.
4. The user decides price. You do not, except **Ratio** (compare their price to accepted value).

## EVALUATE *(workflow — score something that exists)*

Do not fill missing facts. If proof, price, or audience is vague, ask once for a concrete past instance, then score lower and say what evidence would raise it.

1. Intake the artifact in one pass: **offer** — what it is, who, what they get, price, proof; **content** — subject, audience, promise, length, hook; **feature** — what it does, when used, outcome, how invoked, evidence; **idea** — one sentence, who adopts it, what changes, smallest test, adjacent precedent.
2. Score each driver 1–10 on **perceived** value. Quote the band. Cite intake evidence. Caps: pre-launch Perceived Likelihood of Achievement ≤4; idea with no users ≤5.
3. Show the value score and band. If two **vehicles** share the same dream outcome, say which drivers explain the price gap.
4. Stop. Do not open a next step unless the user asks to change one number. If they do, run **Feasibility** first, then the next step only from PATHS.md, then one ≤5 hour action on **one driver**. If they ask what it is worth or what to charge, run **Dollars**.

## Rules

- Tag guidance *(book)* vs *(workflow)* when explaining *why* a move works (division math, perception, status, fast wins, psychological vs logical).
- **Get the bottom to zero** *(book)* — after Dream Outcome is clear, do not camp on bigger claims alone; competitive advantage usually lives in Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice (and making Perceived Likelihood of Achievement *felt*).
- Cite intake or a locked move before stating a score.
- If the user disputes a score, ask what evidence would justify a higher one.
- Design moves name a deliverable, a number, or a deadline. "Improve the marketing" is not a move.
- If the user arrived from **winning-markets** (Value Equation handoff), carry locked **avatar**, **pain statement**, scores on **Pain / Purchasing Power / Easy to Target / Growing**, and **Great / Normal / Bad** market read. Set CREATE and type offer without re-asking. Normal market → **Grand Slam Offer**-level work on the value drivers is required; starving-crowd market still benefits from all four drivers. Do not re-run market pillars unless they ask.
