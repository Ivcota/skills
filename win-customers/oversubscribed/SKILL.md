# Oversubscribed

Daniel Priestley’s framework shows founders how to create more qualified buyers than their business can delightfully serve, select the right buyers, and turn remarkable delivery into the next cycle of demand. It combines seven market principles with the five-phase Campaign Driven Enterprise method.

## Core Principle

Oversubscription is a deliberate and honest demand–capacity imbalance. Define capacity by how many people you can delight, concentrate on a market that values the result, and build visible qualified interest before release. Some willing buyers must miss out; otherwise supply expands to meet demand and margins fall.

The method is not manufactured scarcity or pressure. It depends on real capacity, declared terms, selective release, and delivery worth talking about.

**The foundation:** A business is oversubscribed when “many more people want something than capacity allows for.”

## Scoring

- **Release readiness:** reach 5× capacity in strong signals, 10× in educated or entertained prospects, or 100× in soft interest before release.
- **Remarkable Audit:** score every customer, team, and supplier touchpoint from 1–10; improve one or two weak points each month toward 10/10.
- **Operations:** track recommendation intent; the source warns that an average below 7/10 is incompatible with durable growth and profit.

## Campaign Driven Enterprise Process

1. Set the honest annual capacity for delighted clients.
2. Define the perfect market and its high-value problem.
3. Design a product-for-prospects and a remarkable core offering.
4. Map the rolling campaign schedule, theme, and timeline.
5. Signal the future release, capacity, timing, and terms.
6. Ask for expressions of interest and make genuine response visible.
7. Educate or entertain interested prospects until trust forms.
8. Measure signal quantity and quality until a release threshold is reached.
9. Select best-fit buyers and use only preplanned release mechanisms.
10. Complete rehearsed sales follow-up while holding price and capacity.
11. Deliver unexpected positive value and improve weak touchpoints.
12. Capture stories and numbers, debrief, celebrate, rest, and revise.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Trying to sell to everyone** | Removes the demand imbalance and weakens delivery | Hold honest capacity and let some qualified buyers miss out |
| **Targeting the broad market** | Pulls the offer toward average value and price | Focus on people who highly value it, can pay, and fit |
| **Asking for the sale before a signal** | Hides partial interest behind a binary decision | Ask for a low-risk expression of interest first |
| **Inventing scarcity mid-campaign** | Looks opportunistic and damages trust | Preplan staged releases, editions, prices, and deadlines |
| **Changing price or capacity during a rush** | Breaks the declared deal and risks quality | Keep the announced terms until the campaign ends |
| **Building hype beyond delivery** | Produces negative stories that promotion cannot offset | Audit and improve the entire experience before scaling demand |

## Quick Diagnostic

| Question | If No | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Does qualified demand exceed honest delight capacity? | No oversubscription | Reduce capacity or build more demand |
| Is the market narrow, able to pay, and eager for the result? | Weak fit | Redefine the market |
| Is one imbalance driver clearly primary? | Blurred position | Choose innovation, relationship, convenience, or price |
| Can prospects see genuine buyer interest without pressure? | Flat environment | Cluster and display real signals |
| Are philosophy, standards, and limits explicit? | Commodity behavior | State beliefs, terms, and refusal criteria |
| Does an ecosystem lead from low-risk value to implementation? | Commitment wall | Build smaller stairs |
| Is the experience genuinely worth discussing? | Promotion-dependent demand | Invest in remarkable delivery |
| Are capacity, audience, theme, and timeline defined? | Premature campaign | Complete campaign planning |
| Are terms signalled and interested people being nurtured? | Hidden demand | Gather signals and build trust |
| Has signal quantity and quality crossed a stated threshold? | Unsafe release | Continue the build-up |
| Are touchpoints creating unexpected positive energy? | Satisfaction only | Improve weak points and preserve surprises |
| Are stories, results, lessons, celebration, and rest captured? | Repeated mistakes | Close the campaign before starting another |
