# Ethics

Straight Line Selling must be used to help qualified buyers make good decisions, not to pressure bad-fit prospects.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Bad fit:** do not help sell when the offer cannot genuinely help the buyer.
2. **Firm no:** respect a clear refusal. Loop uncertainty, not refusal.
3. **Proof:** do not fabricate credentials, testimonials, case studies, outcomes, clients, or company reputation.
4. **Scarcity/urgency:** do not invent deadlines, limited spots, expiring discounts, or false demand.
5. **Pain:** do not invent, exaggerate, shame, threaten, or weaponize pain. Use only buyer-disclosed pain that the offer can address.
6. **Risk:** do not hide material downsides, complexity, requirements, or implementation burden.
7. **Regulated domains:** scripts must be compliant and risk-disclosing.

## Truth well told

A strong script can be vivid, concise, and persuasive while staying true. Rewrite weak or risky claims by making them:

- specific,
- supported,
- buyer-relevant,
- appropriately caveated,
- free of fake certainty.

## Pressure signals

Stop or pull back when the buyer:

- laughs nervously or calls out pressure,
- repeats the same firm objection without new uncertainty,
- becomes irritated or short,
- withdraws from rapport,
- says no clearly.

Repair pattern:

```text
Please don't misconstrue my enthusiasm for pressure. I believe this could help because of what you told me, but I don't want you doing anything that doesn't feel right. Should we step back and look at whether this is actually a fit?
```

## Ethics audit checklist

For any script or objection response, mark:

1. Claims — true, sourced, or remove.
2. Proof — real and relevant, or remove.
3. Scarcity — real, or remove.
4. Urgency — buyer/context-based, or remove.
5. Pain — buyer-disclosed and solution-relevant, or remove/soften.
6. Fit — clear qualify-out path included.
7. Ask — direct but low-pressure.

## Rewrite examples

Weak / risky:

> This is guaranteed to change your business.

Truth well told:

> If the issues you described are the real bottleneck, this is designed to help you reduce [specific pain] by [mechanism]. The next step would be testing fit, not making a blind leap.

Weak / risky:

> You have to decide today.

Truth well told:

> The reason timing matters is [real reason]. If that reason doesn't apply to you, we should not manufacture urgency.
