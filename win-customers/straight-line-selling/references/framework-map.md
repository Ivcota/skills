# Framework Map

Source: Jordan Belfort, *Way of the Wolf* (2017), local EPUB extraction in `../framework-extraction.md`.

## Core principle

The **Straight Line** is goal-oriented sales communication. From the first words, every seller action should either maintain rapport, gather useful intelligence, build certainty, lower legitimate action risk, reconnect to real pain, or respectfully exit.

The line is not a monologue. When the seller talks, words move toward a decision. When the buyer talks, the seller listens inside useful boundaries: rapport and intelligence, not random tangents.

## Straight Line Syntax

Compact syntax from the appendix:

1. First four seconds.
2. Build massive conscious and unconscious rapport.
3. Gather intelligence.
4. Transition to the body of the presentation.
5. Ask for the order.
6. Deflect and build certainty through looping.
7. Lower the action threshold.
8. Add on pain.
9. Close the deal.
10. Generate massive referrals.
11. Develop customers for life.

## Five-number buying combination

Belfort frames buying as a combination lock with five numbers:

1. Product certainty.
2. Salesperson certainty.
3. Company certainty.
4. Action threshold.
5. Pain threshold.

If a deal stalls, diagnose which number is missing before writing more copy or another rebuttal.

## Three Tens and certainty

The first three numbers are the **Three Tens**:

1. **Product / idea / concept** — buyer believes it solves the need and is valuable.
2. **You, the salesperson** — buyer trusts and connects with the seller.
3. **Company** — buyer trusts the organization behind the product.

Score each from 1 to 10:

- 10 = high certainty.
- 5 = ambivalence / influenceable middle.
- 1 = strong negative certainty.

Build certainty in order: product first, salesperson second, company third.

## Logical and emotional certainty

- **Logical certainty** comes from words: facts, features, benefits, value, proof, risk, fit.
- **Emotional certainty** comes from future pacing plus tonality/body language: the buyer can feel the better future.

Do logical first, emotional second. Logic satisfies the buyer's skepticism; emotion creates movement.

## Action threshold

Action threshold is the level of certainty and safety a buyer needs before acting.

- Low threshold: buyer can act with imperfect certainty if upside is attractive.
- High threshold: buyer needs very high certainty and lower downside.

Lower it by reducing risk/friction and rebalancing the buyer's negative and positive mental movies.

## Pain threshold

Pain creates urgency. Use it only when real and buyer-disclosed.

Use pain in two places:

1. Prospecting: uncover and understand unresolved pain.
2. Later loops: reconnect to the cost of inaction if certainty is high but action is stalled.
