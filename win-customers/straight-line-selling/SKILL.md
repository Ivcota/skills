# Straight Line Selling

Use the **Straight Line**: move only qualified prospects from open to decision by raising ethical **certainty** in the **Three Tens**, lowering legitimate action risk, and reconnecting to real buyer-disclosed pain.

## Universal guardrails

1. Do not help sell to a bad-fit prospect; qualify out respectfully.
2. Do not fabricate scarcity, urgency, credentials, testimonials, outcomes, or company proof.
3. Use pain only when the buyer disclosed it and the offer can genuinely address it; never shame, threaten, or disempower.
4. Respect a firm no. **Loop** through uncertainty, not refusal.
5. If the prospect seems pressured or irritated, pull back, repair rapport, or exit.
6. In regulated domains, keep scripts accurate, compliant, and risk-disclosing.
7. Optimize for good buying decisions, not closing at all costs.

## Choose the branch

1. **Deal Diagnosis** — stalled deal, pipeline coaching, unclear objection. Completion: one bottleneck is named and mapped to a Straight Line lever.
2. **Prospecting** — qualify a lead, build discovery questions, "sell me this pen." Completion: script separates buyers from non-buyers and includes qualify-in/out transitions.
3. **First Four Seconds** — opening line, cold call intro, first meeting frame. Completion: opener establishes sharp/enthusiastic/expert and earns permission to proceed.
4. **Presentation Script** — pitch, demo talk track, close. Completion: script has 1–3 benefit patterns, proof, check-ins, energy-balanced ask, and no fabricated claims.
5. **Looping** — objections, stalls, "think it over," "send info," trust concerns. Completion: each objection response maps to one lever; no canned rebuttal stands alone.
6. **Action Threshold** — buyer likes it but won't act. Completion: next ask has lower perceived downside without misrepresenting risk.
7. **Pain Threshold** — urgency is low despite real pain. Completion: urgency is tied to buyer-disclosed pain and a genuine solution path.
8. **Roleplay** — practice or simulation. Completion: stay in role until user says pause, feedback, switch, or debrief.
9. **Ethics Audit** — manipulation, scarcity, claims, pressure, compliance. Completion: every flagged line is removed, sourced, softened, or rewritten.

## Deal Diagnosis

Load [`references/framework-map.md`](references/framework-map.md) and, for objections, [`references/looping.md`](references/looping.md).

1. Capture offer, buyer, stage, stated objection, last buyer action, and desired next step. Completion: each is known or marked unknown.
2. Score the **Three Tens**: product, salesperson, company. Completion: each score has evidence or a missing question.
3. Assess **action threshold** and **pain threshold**. Completion: hesitation is classified as risk/friction, low pain, missing certainty, or bad fit.
4. Name the single bottleneck. Completion: choose product certainty, seller trust, company trust, action threshold, pain threshold, or qualify-out.
5. Recommend the next move. Completion: output includes a language pattern or discovery question plus stop condition.

## Prospecting

Load [`references/prospecting.md`](references/prospecting.md).

1. Identify buyer, problem, offer, and channel.
2. Define buyer-in-heat, buyer-in-power, lookie-loo, and mistake signals for this market.
3. Build a **sifter** question path from low-invasion to high-invasion.
4. Cover needs, beliefs, past experience, values, ability to buy, and pain.
5. End with both transitions: qualified fit and not-a-fit.

## First Four Seconds

Load [`references/scripts.md`](references/scripts.md); load [`references/tonality-body-language.md`](references/tonality-body-language.md) if delivery matters.

1. Capture channel, audience, relationship, and reason for contact.
2. Draft a concise, familiar, upbeat opener.
3. Establish sharp, enthusiastic, expert without claiming those words.
4. Add justifier and permission request.
5. Add tonality notes only where they change delivery.

## Presentation Script

Load [`references/scripts.md`](references/scripts.md).

1. Gather buyer intelligence first; ask if need, pain, values, or decision context are missing.
2. Build logical certainty: fit, proof, value, risk.
3. Build emotional certainty: future-pace the solved state.
4. **Frame, don't front-load:** use at most three benefit patterns with check-ins.
5. Run the **energy equation** at every ask: energy in must feel lower than benefits out.
6. Ask directly for the order or next commitment.

## Looping

Load [`references/looping.md`](references/looping.md).

1. Classify the response: yes, firm no, or maybe/objection. If firm no, exit respectfully.
2. First objection: acknowledge, then ask a product-certainty question with money aside.
3. If product certainty is low, run a product loop and check for an enthusiastic yes.
4. If product certainty is high, address seller trust, then company trust.
5. Ask again, optionally with a smaller or safer commitment.
6. If still maybe, choose one later-loop lever: action threshold or pain threshold.
7. Watch pressure signals; repair rapport or exit if pressure rises.

## Action Threshold

Load [`references/looping.md`](references/looping.md#action-threshold).

1. Identify the negative movie: what bad outcome is the buyer imagining?
2. Identify the realistic upside movie.
3. Reduce downside ethically: guarantee, trial, cooling-off period, smaller first step, or support.
4. Rebalance the movies in a concise language pattern.
5. Ask for a smaller or safer commitment.

## Pain Threshold

Load [`references/looping.md`](references/looping.md#pain-threshold) and check [`references/ethics.md`](references/ethics.md) if intensity rises.

1. Confirm the pain came from the buyer.
2. Ask cost-of-inaction questions with empathy.
3. Future-pace consequences of no action.
4. Future-pace the pain-free outcome.
5. Ask for the next commitment with low pressure.

## Roleplay

Pick a mode: seller demo, prospect simulation, drill, or transcript coach. Pick an archetype: buyer in heat, buyer in power, lookie-loo, mistake, or high-action-threshold buyer. Stay in mode until the user says pause, feedback, switch, or debrief.
