# Winning Markets — Pillar Rubrics

Score each pillar **1–10** with evidence. Bands are directional; a single killer fact can justify anchoring high or low inside a band.

## Pain

| Score | Band | Signals |
|-------|------|---------|
| 9–10 | **Desperate** | Active search, prior spend, clear failure cost, problem keeps them up at night; *pain is the pitch* if you mirror it |
| 7–8 | **Urgent** | Known problem, allocating time/money, repeating failed fixes |
| 5–6 | **Real but soft** | Acknowledged issue; "someday" or low emotional charge |
| 3–4 | **Latent** | Nice-to-have; no recent attempt to solve |
| 1–2 | **Absent** | No recognized problem; education market from zero |

**Cap:** If the user cannot name a **specific past attempt** to solve it, Pain ≤5 unless they supply evidence.

## Purchasing power *(book — not “Money”)*

| Score | Band | Signals |
|-------|------|---------|
| 9–10 | **High wallet** | B2B or high-income avatar; already pays for adjacent solutions; budget owner or strong ROI |
| 7–8 | **Can pay premium** | Income or business revenue supports four-figure+ offers in this category |
| 5–6 | **Mid** | Can pay hundreds if ROI obvious; price-sensitive |
| 3–4 | **Low** | Consumer stress, unemployment, or hobby budget — resume-service-to-unemployed trap *(book)* |
| 1–2 | **No wallet** | Cannot reach price required for seller's time at desired margin |

**Ask:** "What do they already pay for something adjacent?" No answer → treat Purchasing Power as *(guess)* and score ≤6 until known.

## Easy to target *(book — not “Targeting”)*

| Score | Band | Signals |
|-------|------|---------|
| 9–10 | **Listable** | Named titles, registries, conferences, FB groups, ad interest stacks, clear firmographics |
| 7–8 | **Reachable** | 2–3 reliable channels; identifiable online or offline gatherings |
| 5–6 | **Workable** | Can find them with effort; fragmented but not invisible |
| 3–4 | **Haystack** | No associations, scattered identity, "everyone needs this" |
| 1–2 | **Unfindable** | No practical way to build a list or audience in reasonable time |

## Growth

| Score | Band | Signals |
|-------|------|---------|
| 9–10 | **Strong tailwind** | Category growing fast; demographic wave (e.g. aging cohort) *(book)* |
| 7–8 | **Growing** | At or above general economy; stable spend shift into category |
| 5–6 | **Normal** | Flat macro; still fine with strong offer |
| 3–4 | **Headwind** | Shrinking spend or secular decline |
| 1–2 | **Dying** | Structural collapse (e.g. print newspapers) without temporary spike |

**Spike exception:** Short-term starving crowd (e.g. COVID masks, TP) can justify a **time-boxed Great tier** — note expiry in the brief.

## Composite hints *(workflow only)*

Not a second score — use to sanity-check Step 4:

- **All ≥6, none ≤4** → Normal or Great candidate depending on starving-crowd yes/no
- **Any ≤3** → name the flag; require override to continue
- **Pain high, Purchasing Power low** → charity / grant / B2B buyer path, or wrong niche on the ladder
