# Winning Markets — Niching Ladders

Use when generating Step 2 candidates. Same **core product** can sit on every rung; messaging and price follow specificity.

## Pattern

Each ladder has **three rungs**:

1. **Broad** — commodity pricing, weak "for me"
2. **Mid** — vertical or role defined
3. **Narrow** — role + situation + constraint + outcome *(book — 100× messaging rule)*

Show implied **price band** as *(workflow guess)*, not a promise.

## Wealth — time / productivity

| Rung | Avatar | Implied band *(guess)* |
|------|--------|-------------------------|
| Broad | Anyone who wants time management | ~$19 course |
| Mid | B2B sales professionals | ~$99 |
| Mid+ | Outbound B2B sales reps | ~$499 |
| Narrow | Outbound B2B **power tools & gardening** sales reps | ~$1,997 *(book table)* |

## Relationships

| Rung | Avatar | Note *(book)* |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Broad | People who want better relationships | Low ticket |
| Mid | Dating coaching | Mid |
| Narrow | **Second-half-of-life** relationship coach for seniors | More pain + more purchasing power vs college students |

## Anti-patterns (kill list)

- "Entrepreneurs," "SMBs," "coaches," "consultants" without vertical
- "Everyone who…" / "anyone struggling with…"
- Niches defined only by **your** interest, not avatar pain + wallet

## Pivot prompts when stuck

- Add **employer** — who signs the check if the user isn't the buyer?
- Add **situation** — new job, post-divorce, first child, lease signed, diagnosis, quota miss
- Add **failed alternative** — "tried X and failed" defines pain and copy
