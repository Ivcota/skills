# Grand Slam Offer — Patterns & Examples

Per-step copy patterns, naming templates, and worked examples drawn from Hormozi's *$100M Offers*. Skill loads this when generating candidates so output stays book-faithful instead of drifting to generic LLM phrasing.

The running example throughout is **weight loss for busy moms** — Hormozi's canonical book illustration — so each step's exemplar threads cleanly into the next.

---

## Step 1 — Dream Outcome

**Strong vs weak (in customer's voice):**

| ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---|---|
| "More clients" | "Land 10 new $5K consulting clients in 90 days" |
| "Get healthier" | "Lose 20 lbs in 12 weeks without giving up wine or carbs" |
| "Improve sales" | "Close 3 enterprise deals/quarter without a sales team" |
| "Be a better leader" | "Run a 50-person org without working past 6pm" |
| "Grow my SaaS" | "Hit $30K MRR within 6 months from cold outbound only" |

**Pattern:** `[Specific result] in [time] without [common sacrifice]`

**Anti-pattern:** abstract verbs ("grow," "improve," "scale") without a number, a deadline, or a named sacrifice removed.

## Step 2 — List Problems (Weight-Loss Exemplar)

Hormozi's canonical problem list — use as the calibration target for completeness and detail.

**Stage 1: Buying healthy food (grocery shopping)**
- E&S: hard, confusing, won't like it
- T: takes too much time
- DO: too expensive, not financially worth it
- L: I won't be able to keep doing it; family's needs will get in my way; can't do it while traveling

**Stage 2: Cooking healthy food**
- E&S: hard, won't enjoy it, will suck at it
- T: takes too much time
- DO: too expensive, not worth it
- L: unsustainable; family will derail me; can't cook healthy while traveling

**Stage 3: Eating healthy food**
- E&S: doesn't taste good; complicated to follow
- T: meal prep eats my evenings
- DO: cost of eating out healthy is steep
- L: can't sustain; social events break me

**Stage 4: Exercising regularly**
- E&S: hard, intimidating, I'll suck at it
- T: no time in my schedule
- DO: gym membership and gear cost too much
- L: I always quit by week 3

**Pattern:** every stage gets all 4 drivers (DO/L/E&S/T). When generating for a new domain, walk the same matrix per stage.

## Step 3 — Solutions (Flip Patterns)

Mechanical reversal: every problem → "How to..." statement.

**Reversal templates by driver:**

| Problem driver | Flip template |
|---|---|
| **E&S** ("too hard, confusing") | "How to make [X] easy and enjoyable, especially for [avatar]" |
| **T** ("too slow") | "How to [X] in under [tight time]" |
| **DO** ("not worth it / too expensive") | "How [doing X] is actually cheaper than [status quo]" |
| **L** ("won't work for me") | "How to make [X] stick despite [specific constraint]" |

**Worked flips (paired with Step 2):**

- "Buying healthy food is hard" → "How busy moms can buy healthy food without confusion"
- "Cooking healthy takes too long" → "How to cook healthy meals in under 5 minutes"
- "Eating healthy is expensive" → "How healthy eating actually costs less than your current grocery bill"
- "I always quit by week 3" → "How to make exercise stick when motivation runs out"

**Solution naming pattern (3-7 words):**
- "5-Minute Healthy Meals"
- "Travel-Proof Eating Playbook"
- "No-Willpower Exercise System"

## Step 4 — Trim & Stack (Quadrant Examples)

| Quadrant | Example (weight loss for busy moms) |
|---|---|
| **ANCHOR** (Hi-Val, Hi-Cost) | Live weekly group coaching with the founder — high cost to deliver, but signals the program is serious; justifies the headline price |
| **CORE** (Hi-Val, Lo-Cost) | Pre-recorded recipe video library; meal-plan generator app; weekly habit-tracker template |
| **DEMOTE** (Lo-Val, Lo-Cost) | Generic motivational PDFs; bonus reading list; community emojis |
| **CUT** (Lo-Val, Hi-Cost) | Custom 1-on-1 dietitian calls for every member; in-person retreats |

**Vehicle ladder, increasing perceived value:**
PDF → Video → Software/Tool → Live group → 1-on-1 → Done-for-you

**Vehicle ladder, increasing cost to deliver:**
Async DIY → Software → Group live → 1-on-1 → Done-for-you

The ANCHOR is usually a cell on the right side of the cost ladder where the value is also highest.

## Step 5 — Bonuses (Copy Patterns + Examples)

**Pitch-pattern per bonus** *(book — rule 3 from Hormozi's bonuses chapter):*

```
Bonus #N: [Name] — a $[Value] value
This solves: [specific objection it kills]
What it is: [one-sentence what]
Why it exists: [how you discovered/created it — short story]
What it does for you: [vivid future picture]
Proof: [stat / case study / anecdote, if available]
```

**Stack reveal copy patterns:**
- "But wait — that's not all. You also get..."
- "Bonus #1: [Name] (a $X value) — FREE"
- "This bonus alone is worth more than your entire investment"
- "We added this because we noticed [specific objection] was holding people back"
- "Total bonus value: $[sum]. Yours free when you join today."

**Bonus naming examples (book-style):**
- "The Restaurant Rescue Cheat Sheet" (eats-out-too-much objection)
- "The 6-Week Six-Pack Bootcamp for Busy Men"
- "The Pantry Reset Toolkit"
- "The Travel-Day Eating Playbook"
- "The 'No Willpower' Habit Tracker"
- "The Founder's Weekly Office Hours Pass"
- "The Spouse Sabotage Survival Guide"
- "The Plateau Buster Protocol"

**Partner bonus examples:**
- Software trial extended to 6 months (partner SaaS)
- $200 voucher with a meal-prep delivery service
- Free private podcast feed from another creator

## Step 6 — Guarantees (Phrasing by Type)

**Unconditional**
- "Try it for 30 days. Not a fit? Full refund. Keep the bonuses."
- "100% Satisfaction Guarantee — love it or your money back, no questions."

**Conditional**
- "Complete all 12 modules and submit your final assignment. If you haven't [specific result], we refund 100%."
- "Implement the 90-day plan. If you don't land 3 new clients, full refund."

**Anti-Guarantee**
- "All sales final. We only accept members who are 100% committed."
- "No refunds — we're choosing you as much as you're choosing us."

**Performance-Based** *(strongest for B2B / measurable outcomes)*
- "We guarantee 50 qualified leads in 90 days, or we work free until you get them."
- "Hit $10K MRR in 6 months or we refund every penny — and you keep the program."
- "Your first 3 clients in 60 days, or your money back plus $500 for your time."

**Stacked**
- "30-day unconditional refund + 90-day performance guarantee. You literally cannot lose."

**Naming patterns:**
- `[Outcome] Guarantee` — "The 10 Clients in 90 Days Guarantee"
- `The [Branded] Promise` — "The Iron-Clad Pipeline Promise"
- `Double-Your-[X]-Back Guarantee` — "Double Your Investment Back Guarantee"

**Strong vs weak phrasing:**

| ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---|---|
| "Money-back guarantee" | "Full refund + we pay you $500 for your time if it doesn't work" |
| "Satisfaction guaranteed" | "Complete the 12 modules. If you don't [specific result], 100% refund." |
| "Try risk-free" | "If we don't [specific outcome] in [time], you don't pay" |

## Step 7 — Scarcity & Urgency (Copy Patterns)

**Capacity scarcity:**
- "Only [X] spots remaining in this cohort"
- "We limit enrollment to [X] members per quarter to ensure personal attention"
- "Founding-member pricing — locked in for life — first 100 only"

**Bonus scarcity:**
- "First 20 enrollees also receive [Named Bonus] (a $[X] value)"
- "Order in the next 48 hours and we'll include [Bonus]"

**Cohort window:**
- "Next cohort starts [date]. Enrollment closes [earlier date] at midnight."
- "Doors open March 1, close March 8. Next opening is in 90 days."

**Pricing change:**
- "This pricing is available until [date]. After that, the price increases to $[higher]."
- "Lock in $X today. Price goes to $Y on [date]."

**Anti-patterns to refuse:**
- ❌ Countdown timers that reset on page reload
- ❌ Evergreen "limited time" with no real end date
- ❌ "Only 3 spots left!" when supply is unlimited
- ❌ Fake "going away" bonuses always available

**Patterns:**
- `The [Time] [Avatar] [Goal] [Container]`

**Generic-name killers (reject these):**
- "Business Growth Program" — could be anyone
- "Coaching Mastermind" — no goal, no avatar
- "Online Course" — describes the format, not the value
- "The X Method" alone — needs avatar + goal

## Cross-Step: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Targeting "everyone" | Narrow until uncomfortable, then narrower |
| Pricing on cost | Price on Dream Outcome value × 10 |
| No guarantee | Add a specific, named one — even modest is better than none |
| Vague bonuses | Each gets a distinct name + objection-killed + dollar value |
| Fake scarcity | Only ship claims you'll honor 100% |
| Generic name | Run all 5 MAGIC elements, drop weakest |
| Solving everything | Stay anchored to ONE Dream Outcome |
