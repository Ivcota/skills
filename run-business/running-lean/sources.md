## Shared definitions

- **Business model as product:** model the whole business rather than equate the product with a solution. [S1, Introduction, “Part 1: Design”]
- **Demo–Sell–Build:** discover problems, define and demonstrate the solution, obtain buying commitments, then build; a small arbitrary feature release is not sufficient validation. [S1, Introduction, “Don’t Start with an MVP”]
- **Continuous innovation:** repeated modeling, prioritizing, and testing with ongoing customer engagement. [S1, Introduction, “Speed of Learning Is the New Unfair Advantage”]
- **Mafia offer:** a compelling voluntary offer, not coercion. [S1, Introduction, “Don’t Start with an MVP”]
