# Sources

## Primary — Eliyahu M. Goldratt

- **The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement** (1984, with Jeff Cox)
  The original. Novel form. Introduces the Five Focusing Steps, throughput accounting, and Drum-Buffer-Rope through the story of a plant manager. *Read first.*

- **The Race** (1986, with Robert E. Fox)
  Visual treatment of DBR. Faster than *The Goal* if you only want the operational pattern.

- **The Haystack Syndrome: Sifting Information Out of the Data Ocean** (1990)
  The cleanest treatment of throughput accounting vs. cost accounting. Where Goldratt makes the case that traditional accounting is the enemy of throughput thinking.

- **Theory of Constraints** (1990)
  Direct treatment without the novel form. Compact reference to the core ideas.

- **It's Not Luck** (1994)
  Sequel to *The Goal*. Introduces the Thinking Processes (CRT, FRT, PRT, TT, Evaporating Cloud) applied to sales, distribution, and strategy.

- **Critical Chain** (1997)
  TOC for project management. Critical chain vs. critical path, project buffers, no multitasking on the chain.

- **Necessary But Not Sufficient** (2000, with Eli Schragenheim and Carol Ptak)
  Applied to ERP / IT systems. Argues that technology investments fail when they don't address the constraint.

## Secondary / extensions

- **The Phoenix Project** (2013, Gene Kim, Kevin Behr, George Spafford)
  *The Goal* applied to IT and software. Novel form. Explicit DBR mappings to deployment, operations, and DevOps. The most accessible bridge from manufacturing-flavored TOC to knowledge work.

- **The Unicorn Project** (2019, Gene Kim)
  Companion to *The Phoenix Project* from a developer's perspective. Less directly TOC but builds on the same flow ideas.

- **Beyond the Goal** (2005, audio lecture by Goldratt)
  Goldratt extending TOC to retail, distribution, and project work in his own voice.

## Practitioner resources

- **Goldratt Institute / Goldratt Consulting** publications and white papers — the operationalized procedures (especially for Critical Chain) that are scattered across the novels.
- **TOCICO (Theory of Constraints International Certification Organization)** — case studies and practitioner papers across industries.

## How to read in order

If you want a single book: ***The Goal***.

If you have time for two: ***The Goal*** + ***The Phoenix Project*** (cross-domain reinforcement).

If you want the Thinking Processes (Evaporating Cloud, etc.): ***It's Not Luck*** after *The Goal*.

If you're managing projects: ***Critical Chain*** stands alone but is best after *The Goal*.

For accounting / executive context: ***The Haystack Syndrome*** (where the cost-accounting heresy is fully made).
