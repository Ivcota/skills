# Test

**Core concept:** Investigate customer needs, proposed value, and willingness to pay separately. Strategyzer's testing roadmap orders them as circle, square, rectangle. [S6, introductory funnel and sections 1–3]

**Why it works:** This separates interest in a problem from preferences for a solution and evidence of potential revenue. [S6, sections 1–3]

**Key insights:**

- Test whether the imagined jobs, pains, and gains exist before investing in a complete product. [S6, “Test the circle”]
- Investigate which proposed features customers prioritize. [S6, “Test the square”]
- Examine willingness to pay after testing customer needs and proposed value. [S6, “Test the rectangle”]

**Product applications:**

| Question | Source method | What it investigates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the problem interesting? | Keyword advertising | Search and click behavior. [S6, section 1] |
| Which features matter? | Buy-A-Feature | Customer priorities. [S6, section 2] |
