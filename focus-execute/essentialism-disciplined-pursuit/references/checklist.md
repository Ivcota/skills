# Essentialism Checklist

Use this worksheet to apply the skill to a calendar, project, team strategy, role, or personal operating system.

## 1. Define The Object

- What are we evaluating?
- Is it a commitment, project, meeting, hire, strategy, habit, or role?
- Who is affected by the decision?

## 2. Essence: Reclaim Choice

- What am I assuming I "have to" do?
- What am I choosing?
- What am I choosing not to do?
- What is the trade-off?
- Which problem do I want?

Pass condition: the trade-off is explicit.

## 3. Explore: Find The Vital Few

- What question am I trying to answer?
- Have I created quiet space to think?
- What evidence do I have from the real context?
- What are the possible options?
- Which option scores 90+ against the most important criterion?

Pass condition: a merely good option has been rejected.

## 4. Eliminate: Create Space

- What is the essential intent?
- Is it meaningful and measurable?
- What must be removed?
- What no needs to be said?
- What sunk-cost commitment should be re-evaluated?
- What boundary should be set in advance?

Pass condition: at least one nonessential commitment, feature, meeting, or expectation is removed or renegotiated.

## 5. Execute: Remove Friction

- What buffer does this need?
- What is the slowest hiker?
- What is the minimum viable progress?
- What cue can trigger the routine?
- What is important now?

Pass condition: the next action is small, visible, and protected by a buffer or routine.

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-59 | Nonessentialist default | Stop and reframe the decision. |
| 60-79 | Some discernment, weak elimination | Clarify intent and cut scope. |
| 80-89 | Strong candidate, not yet essential | Tighten criteria or renegotiate. |
| 90-100 | Essential | Commit and design execution. |

## Quick Review

Before shipping the decision, answer:

1. Can we say why this is essential?
2. Can we say what we are not doing?
3. Can we identify the trade-off?
4. Can we protect the work from friction?
5. Can we focus on the next important action now?
