# Paths to 10/10

Each lever has a universal ladder. Climb one rung at a time — a 3 cannot jump to a 9. Below each ladder are **type anchors**: how that rung looks for an offer / content / feature / idea.

In **evaluate mode**, use ladders to prescribe the next move from the user's current score.
In **create mode**, use ladders as a **candidate menu** at each lever — surface 2-3 rungs near the target score and let the user pick.

---

## Dream Outcome — path to 10

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Pick a single audience | Choose one segment. Kill the rest. | 1 day | 3 |
| 3 | Get their words | Listen/interview/read 10 of them. Capture verbatim phrases for "ideal end state." | 1 week | 5 |
| 5 | Quantify it | Attach a number and a time frame to the outcome. | 2 hours | 6 |
| 6 | Add emotional layer | Rewrite as: "Go from [painful state] to [dream state]." Name the feeling. | 1 hour | 7 |
| 7 | Add identity layer | Write who they become: "the founder who...", "the parent who...", "the engineer who..." | 1 hour | 8 |
| 8 | Benchmark against best result | Anchor headline on your top 1% outcome, with proof. | 1 week | 9 |
| 9 | Uniquely articulate | Phrase the outcome so no competitor can copy: domain-specific + time-specific + emotion-specific. | 1 day | 10 |

**Why it matters:** Dream Outcome is the ceiling on price/attention/adoption. No other lever compensates for a weak "what."

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** quantified revenue/time/identity outcome ("$10K MRR in 90 days")
- **CONTENT:** the reframe — name the belief shift the audience leaves with ("X is not Y, it's Z")
- **FEATURE:** the verb the user accomplishes in one beat ("ship the deal," "close the loop")
- **IDEA:** the category-resetting claim ("If this is true, [existing thing] is obsolete")

## Perceived Likelihood — path to 10

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Get 3 free users / pilots | Free trial in exchange for testimonials and data. | 2-6 weeks | 3 |
| 3 | Ship 3 case studies / receipts | Names, metrics, before/after, timeline. Publish. | 1 week | 5 |
| 5 | Name your system | Give the process a proprietary name. Document the steps. | 1 day | 6 |
| 6 | Add a guarantee or proof bar | Money-back, satisfaction, performance, or a hard data point on the page. | 1 day | 7 |
| 7 | Scale to 20+ proof assets | Testimonials + case studies + screenshots + data + credentials. Stack. | 1-3 months | 8 |
| 8 | Performance / outcome guarantee | "If you don't hit X, we keep working free / refund double / repay your time." | planning + legal | 9 |
| 9 | Publish running metrics | Public dashboard, success rate, total impact for users. Live. | ongoing | 10 |

**Why it matters:** The audience is always asking "will this work for *me*?" Every rung lowers that doubt.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** testimonials, case studies, money-back / performance guarantees
- **CONTENT:** show your work — data tables, source links, real screenshots, "I tested this on N tries" credibility
- **FEATURE:** in-product proof — sample data, success stories visible, live counters, "10K teams use this"
- **IDEA:** signed LOIs, waitlist count, pilot results, adjacent precedents

## Time Delay — path to 10 (higher = faster)

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Design a "Day 1 Win" | One tangible thing the user gets in the first session/day. | 1 week | 3 |
| 3 | Break journey into milestones | Week 1 / 2 / 4 deliverables so progress is continuous, not back-loaded. | 3 days | 5 |
| 5 | Add done-for-them setup | You (or the system) do onboarding/migration instead of the user. | 1-4 weeks | 6 |
| 6 | Ship templates and starting points | Eliminate "blank page" — pre-fill everything possible. | 2 weeks | 7 |
| 7 | Automate non-input steps | Anything that doesn't need their brain — automate. | variable | 8 |
| 8 | Compress Day 1 Win to Hour 1 | Ruthlessly cut everything between commit and first result. | 2 weeks | 9 |
| 9 | Same-session full delivery | User goes from commit to full payoff in a single session. | redesign | 10 |

**Why it matters:** Speed is a denominator. Moving 5→8 here can double the composite.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** first result in days, not months; concierge onboarding
- **CONTENT:** insight in the first 3 seconds; one payoff per minute; no preamble
- **FEATURE:** zero-config, value in 10 seconds, defaults pre-selected
- **IDEA:** smallest test runnable this week (not next quarter)

## Effort & Sacrifice — path to 10 (higher = easier)

| Current | Next move | How | Effort | Unlocks |
|---------|-----------|-----|--------|---------|
| 1-2 | Map every step | List every action the user must take. Count them. | 2 hours | 3 |
| 3 | Replace "learn then do" with "do with guidance" | Live support, concierge, co-working, voiceover walkthroughs. | 1 week | 5 |
| 5 | Provide fill-in-the-blank skeletons | Replace blank exercises with pre-filled defaults. | 1-2 weeks | 6 |
| 6 | Pre-select defaults | Every decision they'd have to make — make it for them with a recommended option. | 3 days | 7 |
| 7 | Done-with-you | You're in the loop; they approve decisions. | operational | 8 |
| 8 | Done-for-you | They provide inputs once; you/system execute everything. | major redesign | 9 |
| 9 | Address identity + social cost | Explicitly remove "I have to become someone else / be seen differently" barrier. | 1 week | 10 |

**Why it matters:** Effort is the other denominator. "Done-for-you" is the single biggest value multiplier — for products, content, anything.

**Type anchors:**
- **OFFER:** done-for-you delivery, zero decisions, single input call
- **CONTENT:** no jargon, no prerequisites, single read, visuals do the work
- **FEATURE:** invisible — works the way the user already thinks; no new mental model
- **IDEA:** test requires no engineering, no new platform, no team change

## Choosing the rung

- Pick the smallest rung above current score. Do not skip.
- Prefer **denominator improvements** (Time Delay, Effort) for compounding impact.
- Prefer **Dream Outcome** fixes if the score is below 5 — nothing else matters until the "what" is clear.
- Prefer **Perceived Likelihood** fixes if there's an audience but it isn't converting — the gap is usually trust, not desire.

## First-week / first-build action rules

The "this week" action (evaluate mode) or "first build action" (create mode) must satisfy all three:

1. **Takes ≤5 hours total** — it has to actually happen.
2. **Produces a visible artifact** — a doc, a page, a draft, a template, a screen recording.
3. **Moves exactly one lever up exactly one rung** — no multitasking across levers.

If the action doesn't fit all three, downscope it.
