## Author

- **Eugene M. Schwartz (1927–1995)** — direct-response copywriter; according to the foreword by Martin Edelston (p. iii), he wrote "advertising copy for the best direct marketers in America"; published *How to Double Your Child's Grades in School* (1964), *How to Double Your Power to Learn*, then *Breakthrough Advertising* (1966); had a stroke in 1978; later became a Boardroom consultant; renowned art collector with wife Barbara (Hans Hofmann, Frank Stella, Donald Judd, Milton Avery; collection partly at Art Institute of Chicago).
