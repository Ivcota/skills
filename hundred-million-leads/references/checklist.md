### 3. Delivery — pick one (or combine for up to 12 magnets per problem)

### 4. Headline test plan (test headline first, then image, then subhead)
Three candidates to A/B:
- A: [____]
- B: [____]
- C: [____]

Headline starter: *"FREE [Type]: How we [Quantified Outcome] for [Specific Avatar in Specific Place]."*

### 5. Consumption formats — single format loses 2–4x leads
Check every format you'll ship:
- [ ] Text (PDF / blog / email)
- [ ] Audio (podcast / MP3)
- [ ] Video (long / short)
- [ ] Live (webinar / call)
- [ ] Interactive (quiz / tool / spreadsheet)

### 6. Value-vs-cost gut check
- Is the magnet good enough that the 99% who don't buy will *praise* you, not trash you? [ ] Yes [ ] No

### 7. CTA = (1) what to do + (2) reason to do it *right now*

- Reason now (pick one):

  - [ ] Urgency: *"Through [Day], I'll throw in a free [Bonus] for anyone who [Action]."*
  - [ ] Make Up A Reason (Langer's *because*): *"[CTA] **because** [any reason]."*

## The Warm Outreach Daily Checklist (p. 108)

| Field | Slot |
|---|---|
| Who | Yourself |
| What | First five free |
| Where | Phone / Email / Physical Mail / SMS / Social DM |
| To Whom | Your Contacts (phone + every email + every social) |
| When | First four hours of your day |
| Why | Get customers or intros |
| How | Personalized message → ACA → "Do you know anybody…" |
| How Much | 100 attempts per day |
| How Many | Follow up two more times after first (once/day for 3 days; once/week if mail) |
| How Long | Until you get customers |

Benchmarks: ~1 in 5 reply → ~1 in 5 of those take free → ~1 in 4 of those convert paid = **~1 paying customer per 100 reach outs** (p. 109).

## The Cold Outreach Floor Rules (p. 188)

- [ ] **Floor:** 100/day minimum, 100 days minimum. *"Don't set a daily goal below 100. And don't stop for 100 days minimum."*
- [ ] **3-step scaffold:**
  1. **Build a List** — Scrape > Buy > Look (manual). Specificity is the edge.
  2. **Personalize** — light research that makes the cold reach feel warm.
  3. **Big Fast Value** — drop a magnet/offer so generous they feel stupid saying no. Bar: blow their minds in <30 seconds.
- [ ] **Multi-channel cadence:** Email first → phone follow-up (*"Hey I'm calling you to follow up about my email"*). Two channels beat one.
- [ ] **Profitability rule:** LTGP ≥ **3x** acquisition cost (p. 180). If below, fix the math before scaling volume.
- [ ] **Reading-level check:** Rewrite scripts at 3rd-grade level (Hormozi saw +50% response, p. 173).

Volume targets: 100 personalized emails/day → ~30% open → ~10% reply → ~3% engaged. 100 calls/day → ~20% pickup → ~25% take magnet → ~4 engaged/day.

## Paid Ads Money Math Diagnostic

Run before scaling spend:

- [ ] **LTGP : CAC ≥ 3 : 1** — yes/no? [____]
- [ ] **30-day payback** (Client Financed Acquisition) — does the customer pay back acquisition cost within 30 days? [____]
- [ ] **Test budget** = 2x cash collected from a customer in 30 days (NOT LTGP). Kill below 1x if zero leads. [____]
- [ ] **CAC vs. industry diagnostic:**
  - CAC < 3x industry average → problem is **LTGP** (fix the business model / offer)
  - CAC > 3x industry average → problem is **advertising** (fix the ad / targeting)
  - *"The cost to acquire customers, between competitors, is much closer than you'd think."* (p. 223)
- [ ] **Three Phases — which am I in?**
  - [ ] Phase 1: **Track Money** (instrumented; not yet profitable)
  - [ ] Phase 2: **Lose Money** (testing; magnitude of wins must cover losses ~10x)
  - [ ] Phase 3: **Print Money** (scale)

Skip-rule: don't run paid ads until warm outreach + content + cold outreach are working. *"Don't gamble money you don't have on a skill you haven't built"* (p. 233).

### Step 2: Find the constraint
Lowest-percent step = the constraint: **[____]**.

A 5-point lift on a 5% step ≈ 2x leads. A 5-point lift on a 50% step ≈ 1.1x. Always attack the smallest number first.

### Step 3: This week's test
Single change to test at the constraint: **[____]**.
Test cadence: one test per week per platform.

Default order: **<$1M annual profit** → More → Better → New. **≥$1M** → Better → More → New.

## Seven Levels of Advertisers Self-Diagnostic (pp. 369–376)

| Level | Headline | Primary Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your friends know about the stuff you sell. | Warm outreach. |
| 2 | You consistently let everyone you know about the stuff you sell. | Warm outreach + post content, every day. |

## Referral Growth Equation Calculator (p. 263)

- % Clients Referred Monthly: **[____]%**
- % Clients Churned Monthly: **[____]%**
- **Compounding Growth Rate** = Referred − Churned = **[____]%**

Interpretation:
- **Negative** → "hamster wheel of death." You must advertise just to stay flat.
- **Zero** → treading water.
- **Positive** → compounding without ads. The product earns its own growth.

Threshold check: Are referrals ≥ **25%** of new customers? [ ] Yes [ ] No
- If no → Level 4 trap. Pause ad scale; fix the product.

## Affiliate Commission Rubric (3-tier, pp. 328–329)

Set commissions as a % of your maximum allowed CAC:

| Tier | Trigger | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Agree** | Affiliate signs up | 25% of max CAC |
| **Tier 2 — Activate** | Affiliate runs a launch | 50% of max CAC |
| **Tier 3 — Sustain** | Affiliate hits steady performance | 100% of max CAC |

- Max CAC I can afford: **$[____]**
- Tier 1 payout: $[____] (25%)
- Tier 2 payout: $[____] (50%)
- Tier 3 payout: $[____] (100%)

**Pro tips:**
- "Sell 3, Get It Free" — pay lower tiers in product (retail value to them, cost to you).
- Never cap payouts (p. 334).
- Affiliate must buy and use the product before selling. *"You gotta buy it to ethically sell it"* (p. 324).

## Common-Mistakes Self-Audit (15 yes/no questions)

Score honestly. Each "no" is a leak.

1. [ ] Am I doing 100 primary actions/day (not 100 over time)?
2. [ ] Am I committing to 100 days minimum on each new channel?
3. [ ] Am I doing warm outreach? (Most operators skip it.)
4. [ ] When I reach out, am I asking *who they know* — not asking them to buy?
5. [ ] Is my lead magnet good enough that non-buyers praise me publicly?
6. [ ] Have I committed to 12+ months of content before judging it?
7. [ ] Is my give-to-ask ratio in content ≥ 3:1?
8. [ ] On cold outreach, am I multi-channel (email + phone follow-up)?
9. [ ] Am I optimizing the right number — LTGP, not LTV, not CAC alone?
10. [ ] Have I run the CAC < 3x / > 3x industry diagnostic to know whether to fix offer or ads?
11. [ ] Did I do warm + content + cold outreach *before* turning on paid ads?
12. [ ] If I'm at Level 4, have I paused scaling to fix the product?
13. [ ] For new channels, am I hiring proven experience (not "potential")?
14. [ ] Have I attacked More on my current channel before declaring my market saturated?
15. [ ] Am I committing to stay on a new channel 3–6 months before quitting?

**Score: ___ / 15.** Below 12 = you have a leak before you have a scale problem.
