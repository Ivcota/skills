# Cost and Value Optimization

Source anchors: Chapter 10, pages 290-294; Chapter 16, pages 482-484.

## TCO Categories

Include:

- Software purchase or subscription
- Hardware or cloud infrastructure
- Implementation effort
- Migration cost
- Maintenance and support
- Patching and updates
- Custom enhancements
- Data center or cloud operations
- Security and compliance
- License renewals
- Administration and support staff
- Training
- Retirement or decommissioning

## Cost Review Questions

1. What is the upfront cost?
2. What is the recurring operational cost?
3. What costs grow with users, transactions, data, regions, or environments?
4. What migration or decommissioning costs are one-time but material?
5. What skills or training are required?
6. What compliance and security costs are attached?
7. What would the forecast look like under low, expected, and high demand?
8. What is the architectural trade-off between cost, performance, reliability, and delivery speed?
