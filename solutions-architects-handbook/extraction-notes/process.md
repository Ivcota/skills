# Extraction Notes: Process

1. Establish the business context, stakeholders, goals, scope, assumptions, constraints, dependencies, and risks.
   - **Source:** Chapter 1, pages 2-4; Chapter 16, pages 482-485.
2. Capture functional requirements and NFRs, especially scalability, reliability, availability, performance, security, maintainability, portability, and capacity.
   - **Source:** Chapter 1, page 2; Chapter 16, pages 485-486.
3. Choose the conceptual architecture and candidate patterns, then make technology decisions with pros, cons, and rationale.
   - **Source:** Chapter 16, pages 483-488.
4. Review performance, security, reliability, operations, and cost as first-class design dimensions.
   - **Source:** Chapters 6-10.
5. Document architecture views: business, logical, process, deployment, implementation, data, operational, and optional physical/network/security views.
   - **Source:** Chapter 16, pages 479-481.
6. Validate the design with POCs, assessments, trade-off studies, testing, failure simulations, and stakeholder review.
   - **Source:** Chapter 16, pages 478-479 and 486; Chapter 8, page 248.
7. Finalize, implement, and maintain the SAD as business objectives, technologies, and external constraints change.
   - **Source:** Chapter 16, pages 493-495.
