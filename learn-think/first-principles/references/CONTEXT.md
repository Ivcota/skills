# First Principles Thinking

This language describes a scalable reasoning approach for moving from an unclear situation to a justified next move. The same concepts apply whether an inquiry is simple and reversible or complex and consequential.

## Language

**First Principles Thinking**:
The practice of building a Working Model by reducing relevant Claims to an appropriate Reasoning Floor and reconstructing a justified Next Move from what survives. Its depth is proportional to the Inquiry rather than fixed.
_Avoid_: Root-cause analysis, atomic decomposition

**Inquiry**:
A bounded effort to understand something well enough to decide, solve, explain, or act. Its boundary is set by its objective, scope, stakes, affected parties, time horizon, and cost of delay.
_Avoid_: Problem, when referring to every kind of workflow input

**Claim**:
A proposition that could be true or false and is relevant to an Inquiry. Each Claim has a basis—observed, sourced, assumed, or derived—and a degree of confidence.
_Avoid_: Fact, unless the Claim has earned that status

**Assumption**:
A Claim provisionally treated as true without enough Evidence to establish it at the Inquiry's required confidence. An Assumption is explicit, challengeable, and never silently promoted to a First Principle.
_Avoid_: Fact, First Principle, Unknown

**Evidence**:
Observable or sourced information that increases or decreases confidence in a Claim. Evidence supports a Claim but does not make it universally or permanently true.
_Avoid_: Proof, except where logical or mathematical proof is meant

**First Principle**:
A scope-bound Claim that is independently defensible, foundational to relevant reasoning, and reduced to the Inquiry's Reasoning Floor. It is not necessarily an atomic or universal truth.
_Avoid_: Basic fact, universal truth

**Constraint**:
A boundary that an acceptable Next Move must respect within the Inquiry. A Constraint limits what can be done; it does not establish how reality works.
_Avoid_: First Principle, requirement

**Criterion**:
A quality used to compare or evaluate possible Next Moves. A Criterion expresses what matters, rather than what must be true or cannot be violated.
_Avoid_: Constraint, First Principle

**Unknown**:
Missing or unresolved information that could materially affect the Working Model or Next Move. An Unknown is made explicit rather than silently treated as an Assumption.
_Avoid_: Assumption

**Working Model**:
The current, revisable representation of an Inquiry's relevant Claims, First Principles, Constraints, Criteria, Unknowns, components, and relationships. It is a useful account of reality, not reality itself.
_Avoid_: Final answer, complete truth

**Reasoning Floor**:
The level of reduction below which further decomposition is not expected to produce a Material Change for the current Inquiry. The Reasoning Floor depends on the Inquiry rather than on the atomic structure of reality.
_Avoid_: Atomic level, absolute foundation

**Material Change**:
A difference capable of changing the preferred Next Move, confidence in that move, or the Risk considered acceptable. Materiality is judged relative to the Inquiry's stakes and objective.
_Avoid_: Any detectable difference

**Risk**:
Exposure to an unacceptable consequence arising from uncertainty in the Working Model or execution of a Next Move. First principles reasoning can make Risk explicit and reduce assumption-driven Risk, but cannot eliminate it.
_Avoid_: Uncertainty, certainty of harm

**Sufficient Clarity**:
The state in which the Working Model supports a justified Next Move and further inquiry is unlikely to create enough Material Change to justify its cost or delay. It is a stopping condition, not a claim of certainty or completeness.
_Avoid_: Certainty, complete understanding

**Next Move**:
The immediate decision, action, test, investigation, or deliberate pause justified by the Working Model. A Next Move may create new Evidence and update the Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Final solution
